Security principles

  • Staff identity and roles must be verified on the server, not in browser code.
  • Require MFA and least-privilege access.
  • Record moderation actions in tamper-resistant audit logs.
  • Never expose passwords, tokens, message contents, or precise device coordinates in routine logs.
  • Location recovery stays owner-only and opt-in; admins do not get routine access.
